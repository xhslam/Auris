# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Auris serves focused learners, writers, and knowledge workers who read, listen, annotate, write Markdown notes, and turn source material into durable understanding. They primarily use the desktop app in sustained study or writing sessions and need the interface to preserve flow instead of demanding constant mode switching.

## Product Purpose

Auris is a local-first learning and knowledge workspace that connects reading, listening and shadowing, Markdown notes, review, and AI-assisted understanding. Success means users can move from source material to a polished, reusable note or publishable article without losing content fidelity, context, or trust in what the interface shows.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and professional. Auris should feel like a mature desktop instrument: focused enough for long sessions, restrained enough to disappear behind the work, and trustworthy enough that previews and exported results can be treated as authoritative.

## Anti-references

- Do not copy another product's brand, logo, proprietary illustrations, or distinctive visual assets.
- Do not resemble a noisy AI dashboard full of decorative cards, oversized hero metrics, saturated inactive states, or gratuitous motion.
- Do not preserve the repository's historical mix of unrelated visual systems when it makes one workflow feel fragmented.
- Do not show controls, previews, or export states that promise behavior the implementation cannot faithfully deliver.

## Design Principles

1. **The rendered result is the contract.** Preview, copy, export, and the destination surface must agree.
2. **Keep the author in flow.** Editing, reading, and verification should require minimal mode switching and update without perceptible lag.
3. **Earn trust through truthful states.** Controls and status copy must reflect actual capabilities, failures, and persistence.
4. **Preserve content before decoration.** Markdown semantics, document integrity, and accessibility outrank visual novelty.
5. **Use one product vocabulary.** Reuse Auris layout, controls, tokens, and interaction patterns unless a workflow-specific surface requires deliberate isolation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for text contrast, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, and semantic labels. Respect reduced-motion preferences, do not encode state by color alone, preserve zoom and text scaling, and keep dense writing surfaces legible for extended use.
